## Users

**Primary:** Idosos (65+) seeking personalized training to maintain independence, strength, balance, and quality of life. May access directly or via referrals from family members seeking care.

**Secondary:** Filhos/cuidadores searching for qualified personal training services for elderly parents; adults (30-60) interested in preventive health; corporate programs seeking wellness solutions.

## Product Purpose

Personal training website for Nathalia de Miranda Souza, a specialist in senior fitness with 20+ years of experience. The site exists to:
- Establish professional credibility and trust through biography and experience showcase
- Generate leads and direct inquiries via WhatsApp and contact form
- Educate visitors about benefits of personalized senior training
- Convert visitors into clients by demonstrating expertise and human-centered approach

Success = consistent lead generation (WhatsApp inquiries, contact form submissions) with strong perception of professional authority and personalized, science-based coaching.

## Positioning

Nathalia's unique positioning: certified Physical Education specialist with 20+ years across schools, gyms, corporate wellness, and now dedicated senior training. Deep respect for individual life stories, science-based exercise physiology, and focus on functional independence (not just fitness). Tagline: "Cuidar da sua saúde é a minha missão. Promover qualidade de vida é o meu propósito."

Differentiation: Humanized, individualized approach grounded in exercise science—not templated fitness.

## Product Principles

1. **Trust through expertise:** Communicate 20+ years of specialized knowledge and continuous professional development; ground every claim in exercise science, not trends.

2. **Humanized service:** Emphasize individualized assessment, respect for life history, and person-centered program design; never templated or one-size-fits-all messaging.

3. **Functional excellence:** Frame training around real-world capability (strength, balance, independence, daily activity) not aesthetic or performance metrics.

4. **Lead-generation clarity:** Make WhatsApp contact effortless and visible throughout; remove friction from inquiry path; encourage exploration without gatekeeping content.

5. **Visual professionalism:** Modern, clean design with real photography (user + stock) to establish credibility; avoid amateur or template-heavy appearance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

**Product-specific:**
- Content must be accessible to older adults (WCAG AAA contrast, readable font sizes, touch-friendly interactive elements, clear language)
- Site must perform well on slower connections and older devices (many senior visitors on older hardware)
- Form inputs: 16px minimum to prevent mobile zoom, clear labels, accessible validation messages
- Navigation: clear, predictable, semantic HTML; keyboard navigable throughout
